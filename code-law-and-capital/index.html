Code determines capability. Law determines authority. Capital distributes loss.

Digital-asset systems are technical arrangements, legal orders and structures for allocating capital at the same time. This Codex examines them together, on the evidence, and records where authority, duty, risk and remedy cannot be located.

01 The governing question

The legal question is larger than the technology.

Technical execution cannot confer public authority. Enactment does not prove institutional capability. Market value cannot settle title, legality or remedy. Three questions therefore run through every chapter.

Where has consequential power moved?

Into protocol rules, control points and operational dependencies that no instrument names.

Who remains answerable for its exercise?

Every material public act must trace to a competent institution, a lawful power and a valid instrument.

Can a person still obtain a remedy?

An affected person must be able to know the rule, receive a reason, contest a decision and obtain relief.

02 The Intelligent-Order Test

The thesis is tested through six conditions.

Later chapters record each condition as satisfied, qualified or failed for the issue under examination. The thesis fails for a material field if the controlling asset, authority, duty, risk bearer or remedy cannot be identified after a documented inquiry.

  • Classification legibilityWhether an activity, asset, actor and function can be classified without a product label deciding the answer.
  • Authority traceabilityWhether every material public act can be traced to a competent institution, lawful power and valid instrument.
  • Institutional executabilityWhether the responsible body has the information, coordination route, resources and procedure to perform its duty.
  • Rights and remedyWhether an affected person can know the rule, receive a reason, contest a decision and obtain effective relief.
  • Capital intelligibilityWhether the claimed value, financing route, beneficiaries and allocation of loss are evidenced.
  • Adaptive legalityWhether the order can respond to plausible change without substituting unpublished discretion for law.

04 Doctrine Register

Each doctrine will carry its own record, not merely a mention.

A doctrine is cited across works, so it needs an address of its own: canonical name, authorial definition, provenance and first development, jurisprudential foundation, legal elements, the applicable test, counterarguments, related doctrines, institutional examples, and a status of exploratory, developed, settled or revised.
