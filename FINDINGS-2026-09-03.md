# CRITICAL — fix before anything else

## 1. A literal `97` prints after every cross-reference. All 28 chapter files.

`a.xref::after` renders `content:"\11 97"` — a raw control character (U+0011)
followed by `97`. Every cross-reference displays as **`Chapter 4⁹⁷`**, in gold, at
`.72em`, superscripted.

**In a treatise where superscripts mean footnotes, this reads as a citation to note
97.** Measured: the anchor box is 93.4 px against ~66 px of text.

**Cause: mine.** The intended content was `\2197` (↗). It was written through a bash
heredoc, where `\21` was consumed as an escape — the same class of failure that
earlier turned `\b` into a literal backspace. I recorded that trap and then hit it
again in a different form.

**Fix:** `content:"\2197"` in all 28 `code-law-and-capital/*.html`, plus
`front-matter.html` and `back-matter.html` — and in `tools/build_clean_edition.py`
so it does not regenerate. **Write the patch to a `.py` file. Never heredoc a regex
or an escape sequence.**

## 2. Every footnote marker vanishes in print. 577 orphaned notes.

`tools/build_clean_edition.py`, print block lines 589–606. Line 592 hides
`button.fnbtn`; line 600 styles `sup.fnref a`. But the page's own JS replaces every
`sup.fnref > a` with a `button.fnbtn` at load.

Measured live after JS: `sup.fnref a: 0`, `button.fnbtn: 28`.

So line 600 matches nothing and line 592 hides all markers. **Every printed chapter
carries a footnotes section with nothing in the text pointing to it.** Across the
corpus, 577 notes. For a legal treatise this is disqualifying.

```css
button.fnbtn{display:inline!important;background:none;color:var(--ink);
  border:0;padding:0;font:inherit;font-size:.7em;vertical-align:super}
```
and remove `button.fnbtn` from the line-592 hide list. **Verify by counting markers
under print emulation, not by eye.**

## 3. The platform home's only call-to-action is invisible.

`.btn`, `.btn.primary` and `.herofoot` are used in `index.html` but never defined

# Reading comfort — the largest single defect

**The chapter measure is 107.3 characters per line.** Measured at both 1280 px and
1440 px: `.ptext` computes to 792 px at 17 px Spectral. It is fixed at every
viewport, because `.shell{max-width:76rem}` minus a 17rem sidebar minus
`.sheet{padding:3rem 3.4rem}` minus `.para{padding-left:2.6rem}` lands at 792 px.

Comfortable long-form is 45–85 ch. At 107, over 8,370 words and a 21,406 px
document, return-sweep errors compound. **This is why the chapters feel heavy.**

**Fix: `.ptext{max-width:34rem}`** → measured 73.7 ch. Do *not* narrow `.sheet` —
leave tables and figures their full width with `.tablewrap,figure{max-width:none}`.
`line-height:1.65` becomes correct at that measure.

Platform pages: body is 15.2 px, too small for an academic publisher (Brill uses
16 px). `.lede` renders at 81.3 actual characters despite `max-width:62ch`, because
`ch` in Aptos is narrower than the CSS unit assumes. Set `body{font-size:1rem}`,
`.lede{max-width:56ch}`, `.standfirst{max-width:52ch}`.

# The two design families — resolved

The agent's verdict, which I accept: **keep both, but the split is drawn in the wrong
place.**

The legitimate distinction is *navigating the estate* vs *inside a specific work* —
which is exactly what Brill does. What makes it read as incoherent is that the split
is currently carried by **five variables at once**: ground, body face, display face,
ink and masthead.

**Reduce it to two.** Typeface (Spectral/Cormorant/Archivo inside a work;
Aptos/Georgia in the chrome) and masthead (green inside; cream outside). **Unify
ground, ink, rule and gold across everything.** The chapter identity then reads as an
*edition*, which is what it is — not as a different website.

## The missing metadata

The `<head>` emits only charset, viewport and title — **no `citation_*` tags, so
Google Scholar cannot index a single chapter.** For a work seeking citation this is
the costliest omission.

**No cut-off date is recorded anywhere** — the single most important missing field in
a legal work. Only the Author can supply it.

**DOI:** Crossref is ~$275/yr. **Zenodo mints DOIs free**, with version-level DOIs
that match "v0.25" exactly. Recommended. Do not invent an identifier scheme of your
own — a self-assigned ID no resolver knows is not a persistent identifier, and
claiming otherwise breaches the governance rules.

**Citation form** should cite to **paragraph, not page** — 3,691 stable paragraph
numbers, identical across HTML, PDF and EPUB. This is a genuine architectural
advantage nobody is using.

## Access model — state the constraint honestly

There is no authentication and no payment processing, and none is proposed. Every
tier is a **published intention, not an enforced control**. Anything gated is gated
by not publishing the file.

Do **not** build an "Open Access" page — Brill needs one because most of its
catalogue is closed. This corpus is entirely open; say it once on the title page.
