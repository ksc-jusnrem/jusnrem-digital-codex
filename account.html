An account, and what it is for

The Codex is open. Every chapter, the Doctrine Register, the Table of Authorities and search are free to read, without an account and without registration. An account exists so that the work can reach you when it changes.

What an account gives you

  • Revision noticesNotification when a chapter is revised, with a statement of what changed and why. The published text is never silently overwritten.
  • Update briefingsLegislative and regulatory developments recorded against the propositions they affect.
  • Licensed editionsIn time, the route to downloadable and institutional editions. Not yet available.

What it does not do

  • No paywallNothing in the Codex is behind this account today. Signing in unlocks nothing you cannot already read.
  • No trackingYour reading is not recorded against your identity, and reader data is not sold.
Stated plainly

This registration is for revision notices and for a future licensing route. It is not an access control, and it should not be described as one. Where a work is open, an account is a courtesy to the reader, not a condition of reading.
